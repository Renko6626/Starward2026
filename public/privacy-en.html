STARWARD PILGRIMAGE / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: October 10, 2026

This policy applies to the event website, account sign-in, creator workspace, registration, relay scheduling and work display services provided by Starward Pilgrimage (Starward2026) at hifuu.moe. The operator and party responsible for processing personal information is the Starward Pilgrimage Organizing Committee (“we” or “us”).

You can browse public pages without signing in. When you create an account, register for the event or use other features, we process the relevant information only for the purposes described below. Available third-party sign-in methods are shown on the account access page. The Google data provisions below apply if you choose to sign in with Google.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: Account identifiers, sign-in email and verification status, nickname, profile picture if provided, and account creation and update records. If you set a password, we store its hash. Email verification codes are used for registration, sign-in or password resets.
  • Third-party sign-in information: See the next section for data received when you choose Google sign-in. When you choose QQ sign-in, we receive the identity identifier (OpenID), nickname, profile picture and necessary authorization information provided by QQ to this app. QQ sign-in does not supply a contact email; you enter event contact details separately.
  • Event information: The public credit name, anonymous display choice, Bilibili profile or UID, primary contact details and channel (such as QQ, WeChat or email), optional backup contact details, participation format, creative plan, work information and links, messages to the committee, schedule and collaboration information you provide.
  • Operational and security records: Application and work review results, information changes and scheduling operations, the version and time of your agreement to the activity rules, request times, IP addresses, browser information, sign-in sessions and necessary error records, for account security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
  • Cookies and browser storage: Necessary cookies maintain sign-in sessions and complete authorization verification. A language-preference cookie remembers your chosen interface language. Browser session storage temporarily saves unsubmitted registration forms and selected release times so they are not lost when you change pages. You can clear this data, but may need to sign in again or re-enter a form. We do not use Google sign-in information for advertising.

2. Access to and use of Google user data

Google is used only for basic account sign-in. When you choose Google sign-in and authorize access, we receive your unique Google user identifier to identify your account, together with the email address, email verification status, name or display name, and profile picture provided under that authorization. Requested permissions are limited to basic identity, email and profile information (openid, email and profile). We do not ask for your Google password or request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Calendar, Contacts or other Google Workspace content.

We use this data to verify your identity, create or identify your account on this website, maintain your sign-in session and display basic information in the account interface. We retain account associations and necessary identity information. If authorization tokens and expiry information needed for sign-in verification are stored, they are used only for authentication and account association, not to read other Google services. Google sign-in does not automatically make your email public or use your Google name as your public event credit name. You enter your event credit name in your registration details.

Google user data is processed only by staff who need it to handle account matters and providers who need it to supply hosting, security and account services, as detailed in section 4. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, data trading, credit assessment or training general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Starward Pilgrimage’s use of information received from Google APIs, and transfer of that information to other apps, will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Starward Pilgrimage uses Google only for basic sign-in. We access your Google account identifier, email address, email verification status, and available name and profile picture to authenticate you and manage your account. We do not request Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or other Google Workspace content. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. How we use information

We use account information to provide sign-in and the creator workspace; registration, contact and work information to communicate with creators, review submissions, arrange the relay and collaboration, show progress and make works public according to the event arrangements; and security and operation records to limit abnormal requests, handle disputes, recover from errors and maintain the service. The email service sends verification codes for registration, sign-in and password resets, and is not used for unrelated marketing.

Publication of the commemorative book and permission to use works in connection with it are discussed and confirmed separately. Creating an account or submitting an event application does not itself grant that permission. See the activity rules.

4. Sharing, transfers and public display

  • Organizing committee: Necessary staff access non-public information as needed for event communication, review, scheduling, account support and security handling.
  • Service providers: Cloudflare provides website hosting, database and security services, processing account, event and request data necessary for those services. When Turnstile verification is enabled, it also processes verification tokens and security information such as IP addresses. Resend processes the recipient email address and verification-email content when you request an email verification code. Google or QQ processes authentication requests when you choose the corresponding sign-in method. We transmit only the information needed to provide the relevant features.
  • Public event information: Public credit names, the schedule and approved formal work information are displayed according to event arrangements. Sign-in emails, primary and backup contact details, historical registration links, messages to the committee and internal review notes are not public. Choosing anonymous display affects only your public credit name on this website. The committee can still view your information, and external work links may reveal the creator’s identity.
  • Relay coordination: Your Bilibili profile or UID may be provided to neighboring creators with confirmed release slots solely for relay coordination. This contact entry does not provide email addresses or other contact details.
  • Necessary disclosure: We disclose relevant information only when strictly necessary to fulfill applicable legal obligations, handle security incidents or protect users’ lawful rights and interests, and limit the scope of disclosure. Sharing for other purposes requires a separate explanation and any necessary consent.

We do not sell personal information or provide it to unrelated parties for marketing. When you follow links to external works or third-party services, the other websites’ processing activities are governed by their own policies.

5. Storage and protection

Account and event information is stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare. Necessary operational logs are processed by the hosting provider. Providers may process data outside your region. We transmit information over HTTPS, limit access through sign-in and administrator permission checks, and protect accounts using password hashing and request rate limits. Non-public information is not provided through public work interfaces. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you notice something unusual, contact us as described in section 9.

6. Retention periods

Account information and associated Google or QQ identity information are retained for as long as needed to provide account services. Event registration, contact details, review and operation records are retained for as long as needed to organize the event, maintain subsequent contact, handle disputes and fulfill applicable obligations. Public credit names, the schedule and work information may remain displayed as an event record. You may request their removal.

Sign-in sessions on this website are valid for 30 days and may be renewed during use. Email verification codes are valid for 10 minutes. Expiry of credentials does not mean an account or its historical records have been deleted. Registration information temporarily stored in the browser is cleared after successful submission; you can also clear it through your browser’s site-data controls.

Personal information that is no longer needed should be deleted or de-identified. When you request deletion, we verify account ownership, address account associations, non-public information and the scope of public display, and inform you of the result. If some records must be retained for legal obligations, security or dispute handling, we explain the reason and scope of retention and restrict their use. Copies in backups or provider logs may be cleared according to their respective retention cycles. We do not promise that all copies will disappear immediately.

7. Corrections, revoking authorization and account deletion

You can correct editable event information on the creator profile page. To access your personal information, correct information you cannot edit yourself, delete your account and associated personal data, remove a Google account association or take down public information, contact the committee privately as described in section 9. Specify your account on this website and what you want addressed. We ask only for information necessary to verify the request, never your password or verification code.

You can also revoke this app’s access in your Google Account third-party connection settings. Revoking authorization affects future Google sign-in but does not automatically delete account or event information already stored on this website. To delete that information, you must make a separate request to us. If you have no other available sign-in method, contact the committee about account access before revoking authorization.

Deleting your account or necessary registration information may prevent us from continuing to provide the creator workspace, registration or scheduling services. Content already published on external platforms or independently saved by other people may require separate requests to those platforms or people.

8. Policy updates

We publish updates on this page and revise the effective date. If the purposes for using Google user data, sharing arrangements or other important processing practices change, we explain the changes before implementation through appropriate means such as a website notice, and seek consent again where necessary.

9. Contact us

Contact for privacy questions and data requests: Starward Pilgrimage Organizing Committee. Contact a group administrator through the event QQ group 1078039621 and discuss privacy, account or deletion requests privately. This contact is also available if you cannot sign in to this website. Do not post email addresses, contact details, authorization tokens, passwords or verification codes in the group.
