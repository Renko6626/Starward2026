Starward Pilgrimage activity rules. Last updated: 2026-10-12. Version: 2026-10-12-v13

The complete current activity rules for Starward Pilgrimage appear below and match the activity rules page. Read them before registering. After agreeing, return to the account page to continue registration.

1. Theme and participation 2. Commemorative book, rights and income 3. Generative AI restrictions 4. Content requirements 5. Work formats and submission requirements 6. Completion, submission and late arrangements 7. Work review 8. User information and privacy

1. Theme and participation

Starward Pilgrimage is a fan creation relay themed around the Hifuu Club members: Sumireko, Renko and Maribel. Artwork, fan fiction, videos and other forms of work are welcome.

Register on this website, submit your completed work and its details, and publish it at your agreed release time. There is no limit on the number of participants. If you wish to submit more than one work, or additional slots are needed because the schedule is full, contact the organizing team in the event QQ group (1078039621) to arrange your work and release slots.

2. Commemorative book, rights and income

The organizing team plans to produce a commemorative book. After formal submission, publication, inclusion of works and permissions for their use will be discussed with each creator and confirmed separately. Creating an account, registering or submitting a relay work does not automatically authorize the organizing team to include, print or distribute that work in the book.

Copyright and related rights remain with their original holders. Participation in the relay and publication in the book are confirmed separately. Use of a work in the book requires the creator’s separate consent and must stay within the agreed permission. Any use beyond that scope requires further agreement with the creator.

The book may be sold, with no intention of making a profit in principle. The production scale, price, cost accounting and handling of income or any surplus have not yet been decided. These arrangements will be discussed and confirmed after formal submission. Agreeing to these event rules does not mean agreeing to those undecided arrangements. The final arrangements will be announced after consultation.

3. Generative AI restrictions

Works primarily consisting of content generated by generative AI are prohibited. This applies to images, writing, audio, video and all other submission formats.

4. Content requirements

Shipping works are welcome. This event does not accept works depicting romantic relationships between Hifuu members and original male characters, unnamed male characters or male reader-insert characters. Male characters appearing in supporting roles or in non-romantic interactions are not restricted by this rule.

Works must not contain explicit sexual content (R-18), graphic gore (R-18G) or explicit sexual innuendo.

Content contrary to public order and accepted standards of conduct, or clearly insulting, degrading or intended to provoke hostility, is prohibited. Avoid portrayals that seriously depart from the characters’ usual characterization, or that may cause significant controversy or be unsuitable for public display at this event.

5. Work formats and submission requirements

All submissions must comply with the event theme, content requirements and generative AI restrictions.

Artwork: no restrictions on dimensions or creative medium. Both traditional and digital artwork are accepted. Consider the printing and layout needs of the commemorative book where possible.

Fan fiction: no restrictions on form; length must be 500–50,000 characters.

Fan fiction publication restriction: do not publicly publish your work on other platforms or in fan publications before your agreed relay release time. Public release includes posting the text on platforms such as Bilibili or LOFTER, printing it in a fan publication, or making the full text or substantial content publicly accessible through links or shared drives. Announcing only a title or a description without the work’s text, and privately submitting it to the organizing team for review, do not count as public publication.

Video: no restrictions on duration or creative format.

No duplicate submissions: the same work must not be submitted concurrently or repeatedly to both this relay and other calls for submissions or relay events. Publishing the same work on multiple platforms after its agreed release time does not count as a duplicate submission.

6. Completion, submission and late arrangements

The process consists of registration, work submission and entering work details. At registration, provide your basic information and creative plan. You may choose and reserve a preferred release slot or let the organizing team assign one. Once the work is complete, formally submit its content and enter its details for content review and final confirmation by the organizing team. Registration information does not replace formal work submission. There is no separate work preview stage.

Complete and submit your work by November 11, 2026 at 23:00 Beijing time (UTC+8). In principle, all work information should be complete before this deadline.

Submissions are expected to open two weeks before the relay begins. Completed works can be submitted early once it opens. Submission instructions and review arrangements will be announced in the creator workspace and the event QQ group.

If your work is nearly complete at the deadline, you still need to work on it on November 12, 2026, and you can complete and submit it at least three hours before your own release time, contact the organizing team in the event QQ group to request a late arrangement. Creators with early release slots may be unable to meet both conditions. Any arrangement must be confirmed by the organizing team.

7. Work review

The organizing team will review formally submitted work content as submissions arrive, considering the event theme, content requirements, generative AI restrictions and suitability for public display, and will give final confirmation. Approval of registration information or reservation of a release slot does not mean that the work’s content has been approved.

For works that do not meet the requirements, the team may request changes, reject the submission or ask for its withdrawal, and will explain the reason to the creator.

The organizing team is responsible for explaining these event rules. Publication of the book, handling of income and permitted uses of works will be discussed with participants as set out in these rules.

8. User information and privacy

Create an account using a sign-in method currently available on the website. Registration requires a display name, Bilibili profile or UID, contact method and details, work format and creative plan, for event communication, scheduling and work review. You may choose a preferred release time or let the organizing team assign one. You enter your own contact details, choosing channels such as QQ, WeChat or email independently of your sign-in account. Backup contact details are optional. Briefly describe what you plan to create.

Public display names, the schedule and approved formal work details will be shown according to the event arrangements. Email addresses, primary and backup contact details, historical registration links, messages and internal review notes are not public. Anonymous display affects only your public name on this website. The organizing team can still see your information, and external work links may reveal your identity.

Your Bilibili profile or UID may be provided to neighboring creators with confirmed release slots solely for relay coordination. It must not be forwarded without permission or used for harassment. This contact entry does not provide email addresses or other contact details.

The organizing team will not sell personal information, use it for unrelated marketing or provide it to unrelated parties without authorization. Non-public information is available only to necessary staff for their work. Hosting, security verification and email providers process information needed to provide their services.

The website retains necessary sign-in, security and event operation records. Identity identifiers, necessary authorization information, nicknames and avatars from existing third-party authorized accounts are retained; contact details must be entered separately. Passwords are stored as hashes. You can correct editable information in the creator workspace. If you cannot correct something yourself, wish to delete non-public information or remove public information, contact the organizing team in the event QQ group and discuss non-public information privately. After verifying your request, the team should explain the result and any information that must be retained.

For details on collecting, using, sharing, retaining and deleting account and event information, see the Privacy Policy.
